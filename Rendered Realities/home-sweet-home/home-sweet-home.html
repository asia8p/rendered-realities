Project Statement

This project explores how feelings of home and belonging are formed and shared within a community. Centered on the experiences of both short and long-term residents of Binghamton, the work examines what matters most to individuals about their community and how those attachments emerge through relationships, experiences, and places. Structured around three guiding questions—who, what, and where makes someone feel at home. The project fabricates a stylized rendering of the interior of Stellar Human that will change in response to viewer engagement. The digital space houses stylized avatars, which function as virtual stand-ins.
