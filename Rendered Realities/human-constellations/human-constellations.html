Project Statement

The project Human Constellations serves as the entry piece, welcoming viewers entering the Rendered Realities art installation. Human Constellations aims to explore ideas of movement, proximity, and volume within a space using a live object-detection algorithm. This generative art piece is inspired by the visuals of constellations and nebula clouds. Building on this foundation, each participant is represented as a star in space, where movement and position allow individuals to group together and create constellations that evolve as viewers move through the gallery.
